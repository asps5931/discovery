# Project Overview

## Background

Commerce is currently running on Magento 2.4, which has been increasingly difficult to maintain and customize. The platform's monolithic architecture limits our ability to deliver modern customer experiences.

## Objective

Replatform the storefront to a headless architecture using Shopify Plus as the commerce backend and Hydrogen as the frontend framework. This will give us:

- Faster page loads (target LCP < 1.5s)
- Full design control without platform constraints
- Reduced maintenance overhead
- Better developer experience for the internal team

## Scope

**In scope:**
- Storefront redesign and rebuild
- Product listing (PLP) and product detail (PDP) pages
- Cart and checkout flow
- Customer account pages
- Search functionality

**Out of scope:**
- ERP integration (Phase 2)
- Mobile app (separate initiative)
- Email platform migration
- Back-office admin tools
